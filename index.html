CORE_PHILOSOPHY: ENTROPY_AS_ENGINE

Traditional human engineering attempts to build a fortress against the universe. Projects like Elon Musk's Terafab aim to brute-force orbital manufacturing, launching incredibly fragile silicon chips into space and hiding them behind massive radiation shields. When the shield fails, the system dies.

Project Swarm is the exact opposite.

We do not fight the chaos of the cosmos; we align with it. Project Swarm proposes launching a massive web of raw, programmable Piezo-phononic Smart Rocks into a highly radioactive solar orbit. Rather than avoiding destruction, these nodes require destruction to function. When a solar anomaly crushes a node, it generates raw energy and acoustic data. The universe's entropy becomes our computational engine.
